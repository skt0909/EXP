Bonus Players

TACTICAL RULE
• Pick 2 players from your starting XI matching your active tactic position.
• If a Bonus Player doesn't play, the Bonus is lost and does not pass to an auto or tactical sub.
• Bonus Players are locked (🔒) and cannot be swapped out by a Tactical Sub.
• Attack tactic requires selecting at least 2 forwards in your starting XI formation.
8

Bench & Subs

TACTICAL RULE
Auto Subs (Slots 12 & 13) AUTOMATIC

Covers starters who register 0 minutes. Backup GK (Slot 12) only replaces GK. Outfield Auto Sub (Slot 13) replaces the lowest-numbered missing outfield starter provided the formation remains valid.

Tactical Subs (Slots 14 & 15) BANK & STACK

Pre-planned before deadline for same-position swaps. The incoming player's match must kick off after the outgoing player's match ends. Both players keep points! Outgoing points are safely banked.

Sub Performance Bonus
